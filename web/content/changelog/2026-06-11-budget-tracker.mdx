Tracking what you spent is only half of it. The question people actually ask is
_can I afford this?_ — so Blipko now runs a real budget behind every message.

Every expense is sorted into one of three buckets:

- **Needs** — rent, groceries, bills. Target 50% of income.
- **Wants** — eating out, subscriptions, that impulse buy. Target 30%.
- **Savings** — what you keep. Target 20%.

### New commands

- **`/status`** — where each bucket stands and how much you can safely spend per
  day for the rest of the cycle.
- **`/report`** — a monthly summary of where the money went.
- **Undo** — got a number wrong? Undo the last entry from the chat.

### Nudges, at your chosen volume

Blipko can warn you as a bucket fills up. You pick the dosage — **off**,
**gentle**, **aggressive**, or **relentless** — so it's a nudge, not nagging.

<Callout variant="info">
  Nudges default to **off**. Turn them on from the bot's settings whenever you
  want them.
</Callout>

### Fixed

- Linking a Telegram account to an existing web login now merges the two
  instead of creating a second account, so bot expenses show up in the
  dashboard straight away.
