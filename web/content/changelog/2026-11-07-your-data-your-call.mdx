Your spending history is some of the most personal data you have. You should be
able to take it with you, or make it disappear, without writing to anyone.

### Export everything

**Account → Your data → Export my data** downloads a single file with every
expense, income, category, box, recurring rule and chat message Blipko holds for
you. Sign-in credentials are never part of it.

### Delete your account

**Delete account** on the same card erases you completely: every expense,
message and raw log, along with the account itself. There is no grace period and
no "we keep a copy" — once you confirm, it's gone. Export first if you want a
record.

### The assistant stays in its lane

Ask the bot which fund or stock to buy and it will now say plainly that it isn't
a registered adviser. It can still tell you exactly what you've saved; for
investment advice it points you to a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
