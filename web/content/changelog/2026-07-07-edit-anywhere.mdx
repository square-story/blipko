### Every confirmation shows your budget

When Blipko records a spend it now replies with the category budget _and_ the
bucket budget it landed in — so you learn where you stand without asking.

### Scheduling that respects your timezone

Nudges, reports and recurring charges all fire against your own timezone, which
you can change in **Account settings**. No more 3am reminders.

### Also new

- **Connect Telegram by QR** — scan it from your phone instead of typing.
  The dashboard detects the connection and moves on by itself.
- **Category emojis**, picked from a proper emoji picker.
- A **FAQ page**, and a marketing navbar that goes somewhere.
- Interaction sounds across the dashboard, off by default, with a toggle in
  settings.

### Fixed

- The transaction date filter works in range mode.
- Beating your savings target is celebrated instead of flagged red.
- The Recent Expenses card is usable on a phone.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` throughout.
