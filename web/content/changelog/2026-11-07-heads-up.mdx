A report at the end of the month tells you what already happened. The check-in
is the moment you can still do something about it, so it now says more than a
percentage.

### When will it run out?

If your Wants spending so far would empty the bucket before payday, the check-in
says so with a date: _"At this pace 🎯 Wants runs out around 23 Jun."_ It waits
until the third day of the cycle and stays quiet when you're on track. Needs is
left out on purpose: rent and bills land once a month, so they aren't a pace.

### Nothing logged today?

Small, frequent spends — chai, an auto, a recharge — are the ones that slip
through. If the evening check-in finds no spending you logged that day, it asks (a
recurring charge posting by itself doesn't count as you logging). One
reply like "chai 20, auto 60" logs both.

### Tomorrow's charges

Subscriptions are easy to forget until the money leaves. Any recurring expense
due tomorrow is named in the check-in the evening before: _"📅 Tomorrow: Netflix
₹649."_

All of this rides on the check-in you already get — no extra messages. Set how
often it comes under **Account → Reminder frequency**.
