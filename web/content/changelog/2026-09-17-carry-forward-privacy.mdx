Three things Blipko used to throw away: the money you didn't spend, the
correction you just made, and your privacy the moment someone glanced at your
screen.

### Privacy mode

<Img
  src="/blipko.show.hide.amounts.button.png"
  alt="/dashboard overview with privacy on: every amount blurred, the eye-off icon visible in the top bar"
  width={3840}
  height={2880}
  caption="privacy on: every amount blurred, the eye-off icon visible in the top bar"
/>

Every dashboard page leads with money, so opening it in a cafe or handing someone
your phone shows them your whole financial position. The eye in the top bar blurs
every figure on screen — category names, dates, chart shapes and navigation all
stay readable, so the app is still usable, just not legible over your shoulder.

Click any blurred figure to reveal that one for a few seconds. The toggle also
lives under **Account → Appearance** and in the ⌘K menu, it's remembered per
device, and it's applied before the page paints, so a reload never flashes your
balance.

Form inputs and CSV exports stay readable on purpose — you can't edit what you
can't read, and an export never renders on screen.

<Callout variant="info">
  This is a blur, not encryption. The number is still in the page for anyone who
  opens devtools. It's a guard against someone looking at your screen, and
  that's all it claims to be.
</Callout>

### Last cycle's leftover carries forward

<Shot label="SHOT — Telegram: the cycle report with the carry prompt below it, all four buttons visible" />

Leftover money used to get printed in the cycle report and then forgotten — not
saved, not spendable, just gone at the boundary. Now you get asked once, at the
start of the cycle, where it should go:

- **Savings** files it as a savings expense, optionally straight into one of your
  boxes.
- **Opening balance** adds it to this cycle's budget, so the month you're
  starting is genuinely bigger.

<Shot label="SHOT — the New cycle modal on /dashboard, amount prefilled, a box selected in the dropdown" />

The same question appears on the dashboard, and answering it in either place
settles both — you can't be asked twice, and you can't carry the same leftover
twice. If the suggested figure doesn't match your actual balance, **Different
amount** lets you say what it really was.

Carried money doesn't count as something you earned, so your savings rate stays
honest.

### It remembers the correction

<Shot label="SHOT — Telegram: an edit confirmation ending with the line about remembering chai → Coffee and Tea" />

Correcting *chai* from Snacks to Coffee & Tea five times taught Blipko nothing —
the sixth `chai 30` was guessed with exactly the same information as the first.

Now the correction sticks. Fix a spend's category and the next message using that
phrase is filed the way you said instead of re-guessed. It works per phrase and
in your own script, so `പെട്രോൾ 500` and `चाय 30` each get their own memory. When
you correct from the Telegram buttons, Blipko tells you it's learned it;
corrections from the dashboard — the review inbox, the edit dialog — are picked
up quietly.

### No more near-duplicate categories

<Img
  src="/blipko.show.unused.categories.review.and.deletion.button.png"
  alt="blipko.show.unused.categories.review.and.deletion.button"
  width={3840}
  height={2880}
  caption="Unused categories review and remove from the blipko"
/>

Category lists used to bloat: `Snack` never matched `Snacks`, and the bot was
free to invent a name when nothing fit, so `Food` appeared next to your
`Eating Out`. Now it reuses what you have — case, punctuation, `&` against `and`,
and plurals all match — and it never makes up a name. A category is only created
once you've actually confirmed the spend, so an abandoned message leaves nothing
behind.

New accounts start with an empty list instead of twenty-odd categories nobody
picked; they appear as you spend. Existing accounts get a cleanup card rather
than a silent purge, and anything you've used — or taught Blipko — is left alone.

### Reminders that actually remind

<Img
  src="/blipko.settings.reminder.options.png"
  alt="blipko.settings.reminder.options"
  width={3840}
  height={2880}
  caption="The reminder setting allows you to choose your preferred options."
/>


The frequency setting was decorative. Every level sent at most one burst a day,
and on Gentle you could go a whole cycle hearing nothing at all. A bucket that
crossed 80% got one alert and could then sit at 88% in silence for the rest of
the month.

Now the level means what it says — Gentle once a day in the evening, Aggressive
twice, Relentless three times — and a bucket that's running hot gets raised again
the next day instead of going quiet after one mention.

### Fixed

- **Reminders, cycle reports and recurring charges stop being skipped.** They
  needed to fire in an exact hour, and the scheduler drifts, so they were landing
  on roughly half the days they should have. They now run on a window.
- Saving a bot-created recurring rule from the dashboard no longer drops its
  category.
- The assistant no longer gives up with "I couldn't work that out right now" on
  longer questions.
