Buckets are a good default. But "Wants" covers both your coffee habit and your
gym membership, and you probably want to see those separately.

### Onboarding that sets you up properly

A three-step wizard on the web: your income, the category groups that match how
you actually live, and how loudly you want to be reminded. Blipko builds your
taxonomy from that and you're tracking in under a minute.

### Pin what matters, auto-balance the rest

- **Pinned budgets** stay exactly where you put them. Rent is rent.
- **Auto-balance** distributes what's left across everything unpinned,
  weighted by how you've actually been spending — not evenly.
- **Suggestions** come from your own history, so a new category starts with a
  number that isn't a guess.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Auto-balance never drops a category below a minimum floor, and always asks
  before it changes anything.
</Callout>

### `/report` compares cycles now

Your end-of-cycle report shows deltas against the previous cycle — up or down,
per bucket — and arrives on its own once the cycle closes.

### Fixed

- Group rows no longer show spend or budget of their own, so bucket totals
  reconcile.
- Uncategorized spend is visible instead of quietly missing from the total.
- Early-cycle projections are suppressed until there's enough data to be
  meaningful, rather than screaming about a single large Monday.
- Savings is framed as a goal you're progressing toward, not a budget you're
  breaching.
- `/help` actually explains everything the bot can do.
