You open your banking app and the number is lower than you expected. Somewhere
between the chai, the auto and the recharge, money moved that never got
logged. Now Blipko can tell you how much.

### How it works

Send your total balance — bank, UPI and cash you spend from, added up:
_"balance 12400"_. The first time, Blipko just remembers it.

Next time you send it, Blipko works out what the balance _should_ be — the last
figure, plus income, minus spending you logged since — and compares:

> 🔍 Balance check since 10 Nov
> Started ₹10,000 · income +₹0 · spent −₹50
> Expected ₹9,950 · you have ₹9,500
>
> ₹450 isn't accounted for — usually small cash or UPI spends that never got logged.

One tap logs the gap as **Untracked**, so the books close and the next check
starts clean. If you have _more_ than expected, Blipko says so — usually a
refund or income that wasn't logged.

Each check covers only the time since the last one. Send just `/balance` to see
what it should be right now, and the end-of-cycle report tells you how much
balance checks found that cycle.
