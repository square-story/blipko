A friend paying back ₹1,500. A purchase you returned. A work expense reimbursed.
Every one of those used to widen your budget by that much — even though the
original spend had already eaten it. Money the budget had spent once got handed
back to you to spend again.

### Not every rupee is income

<Shot label="SHOT — /dashboard/categories/income, both cards visible with their cycle totals" />

Income now files under one of thirteen categories, in two groups.

- **Counts as income** — Salary, Freelance, Business Income, Dividend &
  Interest, Rent Received, Bonus, Gift, Other Income.
- **Doesn't raise your budget** — Reimbursement, Refund, Money Lent Returned,
  Loan / Advance Received, Transfer Between Accounts.

The second group is still recorded and still shows in your income total. It just
stops pretending you earned it. The list is fixed rather than something else to
set up, and income you logged before today still counts as earnings — nobody's
numbers moved when this shipped.

### See it, and fix it

<Shot label="SHOT — /dashboard/income with a Refund row and a Salary row, Category filter dropdown open" />

Income has a **Category** column, a multi-select filter, and a picker in the edit
dialog — so a refund the bot read as salary is now a two-click correction rather
than something you live with. It's in the CSV export too.

### The bot tells you which is which

<Shot label="SHOT — Telegram /status reply showing the income basis line, e.g. 'Income ₹69,000 (budget on ₹62,000)'" />

Log a refund and the reply says so: *↩️ Money coming back, not new income — your
budget is unchanged.* And `/status`, `/report` and the cycle report all carry the
same line — what landed, what the budget is actually built on, and the gap
between them, named rather than left for you to work out.

Telegram and the dashboard now quote the same budget. They used to disagree, by
exactly the refunds.

### Recurring income, filed right

<Shot label="SHOT — /dashboard/recurring, rule form open on Income, the grouped income-category select expanded" />

A recurring income rule picks from the income categories instead of the spending
ones. A reimbursement that arrives every month stops inflating the budget every
month.

### Fixed

- **Savings rate** in Analytics and in Wrapped divides by what you earned, not by
  everything that landed. A month with a purchase later refunded used to score
  worse than the same month with neither transaction in it.
- `/report` no longer hides refunds while `/status` names them.
- Income inside a multi-item message gets categorised too — "chai 30, nadha
  returned 1500" comes back as a mixed ✅ / ↩️ list.
