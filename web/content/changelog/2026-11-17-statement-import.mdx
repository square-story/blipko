Chat logging catches what you remember to send. Your bank statement has the
rest. Now you can bring it in.

### How it works

Open **Import** on the dashboard and pick your statement's CSV file (net
banking usually offers it as a download; if your bank only gives Excel, open it
and _Save As_ CSV). HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis and most other layouts are
recognised automatically; if not, you pick the date, description and amount
columns once.

The file is read **in your browser** — the statement itself never leaves your
device. Only the rows you choose to import are saved.

### What gets ticked

- **New spends** are ticked, with the payee pulled out of messy bank text
  (_UPI-SWIGGY-SWIGGY8@YBL-…_ becomes **Swiggy**) and a category if you've
  filed that payee before.
- **Already logged?** Anything matching an entry you already have — same
  amount, within a day — is left unticked, so nothing is counted twice.
- **Credits and cash withdrawals** are shown but unticked. Salary is usually
  logged already, transfers and refunds shouldn't raise your budget, and cash
  is counted when you spend it.

Pick a category for one Swiggy row and every Swiggy row follows — and the bot
remembers it for next time you text "swiggy 250". Changed your mind? The
**Undo** button on the confirmation removes the whole import.
