"Save 20%" is a number. "₹2 lakh for the trip in December" is a goal. **Boxes**
are for goals.

<Video
  id="https://res.cloudinary.com/dfyip7lpv/video/upload/v1785669356/cursorful-video-1785669325524_q9uiwo.mp4"
  caption="Creating a box, then moving a transaction into it."
/>

- **Goal boxes** have a target and fill up as you add to them.
- **Fund boxes** are open-ended — an emergency fund with no finish line.
- **Move money in from anywhere**, including moving an existing transaction into
  a box after the fact.
- **Recurring contributions** so a box fills itself every month.
- Every movement is a ledger entry, so a box can always tell you where its
  money came from — and any move can be reversed.

### Earmarked income

Income can be earmarked straight into a box on the way in. That money stops
counting as spendable, so your safe-to-spend figure stays honest.

A look back at your year: what you spent most on, your biggest month, how your
buckets held up. Export it as an image if you want to share it.

### Also new

- **Per-category breakdown pages** — tap any category to see just its
  transactions.
- **Multi-select filters and column totals** on the transaction tables, so you
  can slice by several categories at once and see the sum.

### Fixed

- Chart bar colours read from the theme tokens instead of hardcoded hex, so
  they're correct in both light and dark mode.
