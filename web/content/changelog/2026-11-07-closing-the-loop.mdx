Most money summaries reset to zero every month. You read them, resolve to "be
more careful", and next month starts from scratch. The cycle report now carries
a thread from one cycle to the next.

### What was unusual

Alongside the biggest movers, the report names any category that ran at least
double its usual — measured against the last few cycles, not just the one
before — so a one-off jump in food delivery stands out from your normal rhythm.
Small amounts are ignored; ₹40 becoming ₹120 is not news.

### One thing to change

Every report now ends with a single, concrete suggestion: bring an unusual
category back toward its usual, keep a bucket within a daily figure, or move a
savings shortfall on payday. One, not five.

### Did it work?

The next report checks that suggestion and tells you plainly: done, or what it
came in at. No storage, no streaks — it is worked out from your own records.

### One tap: how clear does it feel?

Right after the report, one question: how sure are you about where your money
went? Tap 1 to 5. It's how we find out whether Blipko is actually making things
clearer.

### Kinder wording

"Leaks", red crosses and "overspent" are gone. An overrun now reads as
"₹1,200 past budget" — the same fact, without the guilt.
