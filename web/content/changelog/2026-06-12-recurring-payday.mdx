### Recurring rules

Tell the bot `rent 12000 every month` and it becomes a rule. From then on Blipko
posts it on the due date and tells you it did. Works for income too, so your
salary lands automatically.

Manage them all from **Dashboard → Recurring**: pause one, change the amount, or
delete it.

### Your budget follows your payday

Set your payday and the whole budget cycle moves with it. Paid on the 25th? Your
cycle runs the 25th to the 24th, and `/status` paces your daily spend against
_that_ window instead of the calendar month. Payday on the 1st behaves exactly
as before.

### Income, properly tracked

- Income is now first-class: log it by chat, and see it in
  **Transactions → Income**.
- Analytics gained an **income vs spending** view with your net for the cycle.
- Your budget tracks actual income rather than assuming your expected salary —
  floored at the expected figure, so a slow month doesn't shrink your budget.
